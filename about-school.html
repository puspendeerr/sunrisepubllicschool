Welcome to Sunrise Public School

Sunrise Public School is a leading educational institution dedicated to fostering intellectual curiosity, personal growth, and social responsibility in students from diverse backgrounds. Established with a vision to provide holistic education, we empower our students to become lifelong learners and contributing members of society. Our experienced faculty, innovative curriculum, and supportive learning environment ensure that every student receives the guidance and resources they need to excel academically and develop their unique talents. We believe in nurturing not just academic prowess but also critical thinking, creativity, and strong ethical values.

At Sunrise Public School, we strive to create a vibrant community where students feel safe, respected, and challenged. We offer a wide range of extracurricular activities, sports, and clubs that allow students to explore their interests and develop new skills. Our commitment to excellence is reflected in our state-of-the-art facilities, including modern classrooms, well-equipped laboratories, a comprehensive library, and extensive sports facilities. We are constantly evolving to meet the needs of the 21st century learner, integrating technology and innovative teaching methodologies to make learning engaging and effective.

Our Philosophy and Values

Our Mission

To provide a nurturing and stimulating educational environment that inspires students to achieve their full academic, creative, and personal potential. We are committed to fostering critical thinking, problem-solving skills, and a passion for lifelong learning, preparing students to confidently navigate and contribute positively to a rapidly changing world.

Our Vision

To be recognized as a premier educational institution known for our commitment to excellence, innovation, and the holistic development of students. We aspire to cultivate a community of compassionate, responsible, and globally-minded individuals who are equipped with the knowledge, skills, and values to lead fulfilling lives and make a significant impact on society.

Our Values

Our core values of **Integrity, Respect, Excellence, Collaboration, and Compassion** are the foundation of our school culture. We uphold honesty and ethical behavior, promote mutual respect and understanding, strive for excellence in all endeavors, encourage teamwork and community spirit, and foster empathy and kindness towards others.

Our Journey

1990

Foundation and Early Years

Sunrise Public School was established with a foundational commitment to providing accessible and quality education. The initial focus was on building a strong academic program and establishing a nurturing environment for students.

1995-2004

Growth and Expansion

During this period, the school experienced significant growth in student enrollment and infrastructure. New blocks were constructed to accommodate more classrooms, and dedicated science and computer labs were established to enhance learning resources.

2005

CBSE Affiliation

A major milestone was achieved with the affiliation to the Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE). This allowed the school to offer a nationally recognized curriculum and opened new avenues for student assessment and progression.

2006-2014

Academic and Extracurricular Development

The school focused on enriching both academic and extracurricular programs. Introduction of various clubs, sports teams, and cultural events became a regular part of the school calendar, promoting holistic development.

2015-2019

Technological Integration and Modernization

Embracing technological advancements, smart classrooms were implemented across sections. The computer lab was upgraded, and digital resources were integrated into the teaching methodologies to enhance interactive learning.

2020-2023

Focus on Excellence and Innovation

The school achieved consistent high pass rates in board examinations, reflecting the dedication of both students and faculty. New pedagogical approaches were explored, and professional development for teachers was emphasized.

2024

Present Day and Future Outlook

Today, Sunrise Public School stands as a reputable institution, continually adapting to the evolving educational landscape. We are committed to innovation, student well-being, and preparing our students to be successful global citizens.

Our Achievements

Sunrise Public School takes immense pride in the accomplishments of its students and faculty. Our commitment to excellence has resulted in recognition across various domains:

Academic Excellence

Our students consistently achieve top ranks in the CBSE board examinations and perform exceptionally well in national and international academic competitions like Olympiads and quizzes.

Sports Recognition

Our athletes have brought laurels to the school by excelling in various sports at district, state, and national levels. We encourage participation in a wide range of sports activities.

Cultural Triumphs

We have a vibrant cultural scene, with students winning numerous accolades in inter-school arts, music, dance, and theater competitions, showcasing their creative talents.

Community Impact

Our students and staff are actively involved in community service initiatives and awareness campaigns, demonstrating their commitment to social responsibility and making a positive impact.

STEM and Innovation

Students participate and win in various science exhibitions, coding challenges, and technology-related competitions, highlighting our focus on STEM education and innovation.

Global Participation

We encourage participation in international exchange programs and online collaborations, exposing students to global perspectives and fostering cross-cultural understanding.

Campus & Facilities

Our school provides a conducive environment for learning and growth with modern facilities designed to support comprehensive development:

Classrooms

Spacious, well-lit, and ventilated classrooms equipped with ergonomic furniture and smart boards for interactive and engaging lessons.

Laboratories

State-of-the-art laboratories for Physics, Chemistry, Biology, and Computer Science, equipped with the latest apparatus for practical learning and experimentation.

Library

A vast and resourceful library with an extensive collection of books, magazines, journals, and digital resources catering to diverse interests and academic needs.

Sports Complex

Comprehensive sports facilities including a playground for football and cricket, courts for basketball and tennis, and indoor spaces for badminton and table tennis, promoting physical fitness and sportsmanship.

Cafeteria

A hygienic and spacious cafeteria offering a variety of nutritious and delicious meals and snacks, ensuring student well-being.

Transportation

A fleet of well-maintained buses with trained staff provides safe and reliable transportation for students across various routes.

Arts & Music Studios

Dedicated studios for music, dance, and visual arts, providing students with opportunities to explore and hone their creative talents.

Health & Wellness Center

A fully equipped health center with a qualified nurse to attend to the immediate medical needs of students and staff.

Student Life

Life at Sunrise Public School is a vibrant tapestry of academic pursuits, extracurricular activities, and personal growth opportunities. We encourage students to engage in a variety of activities to develop their skills and interests beyond the classroom.

Clubs and Societies

Students can join various clubs such as science, debate, eco, and photography clubs, fostering teamwork and specialized interests.

Events and Celebrations

The school calendar is packed with annual events, cultural programs, and festivals, providing platforms for students to showcase their talents and celebrate diversity.

Excursions and Camps

Educational excursions and camps are organized to provide students with practical learning experiences and opportunities for adventure and bonding.

Leadership Opportunities

Students can take on leadership roles through the student council and various school events, developing responsibility and leadership skills.

Inter-House Competitions

A strong house system promotes healthy competition and camaraderie through various academic, sports, and cultural events.

Community Service

We instill a sense of social responsibility through mandatory community service programs and participation in social awareness campaigns.
